3. Entity-tag retrieval

Short documents lose on embedding distance: a transcribed valve tag is 17 characters sharing no vocabulary with a natural-language question. Indexing the identifiers inside each chunk makes them reachable by name instead.

9. Known limits

Measured and unresolved. Listed because a reviewer will find them faster than they will find the strengths.

LimitEvidenceStatus
Short documents rank poorly on similarity 4 of 23 queries miss at top-5 mitigated by tag filter
Evidence check tests that retrieval returned hits, not that the answer uses them predicate is non-empty retrieval by design
Egress monitor reports connections, does not block them app/network.py deployment enforces
Redaction slows on digit-dense non-text input 8.8 s for 40 KB of hex vs 4.1 ms prose open
Ingest is synchronous; a scan blocks its upload request 77 s for two scanned pages open
Tamper-evident audit ledger — not built

Do not describe the retrieval figure as answer accuracy. 0.826 is the share of queries whose target document appears in the top five results, on a 12-document corpus — not the share of answers that are correct.
